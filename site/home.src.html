The Pung stack

Open music modules, at home on every device.

Pung is {{N:modules}} open instruments, audio effects and MIDI generators that play inside your DAW like its own, with an interface made for the hardware under your hands. One stack for every place you make music, starting with Ableton Push 3 Standalone.

Native in your DAWA module is a device like the DAW's own: in its browser, played, automated and saved with your project. On a Push that is Ableton Live, and nothing new to learn.
An interface made for itEnvelopes, filters and LFOs are drawn as the sound moves, and {{N:custom}} modules have front panels designed for them alone.
Open and freeEvery module is built from its own source and keeps its licence. Pung itself is open source.
