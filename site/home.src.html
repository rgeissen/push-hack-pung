The open instrument platform

Open music modules, at home on every device.

Pung is what makes them at home. It hosts them inside your DAW as devices of its own, gives each an interface built for the hardware under your hands, and lays modulation, sounds and power management over all of them. One stack, built to play the same wherever you make music, starting on Ableton Push 3 Standalone with {{N:modules}} modules.

A host inside your DAWEvery module is a device like the DAW's own: in its browser, played, automated and saved with your project. On a Push that is Ableton Live, and nothing new to learn.
An interface for your hardwareScreens that move with the sound: every note on its envelope, the filter over the live spectrum, each LFO in motion. {{N:custom}} modules have front panels designed for them alone.
Modulation over everythingTwo LFOs, the wheel and pressure on any control of any module, whoever made it, and saved with your project.
Performance, governedPung measures what every module costs and lets an emulator rest when it is silent, so the processor and the battery go to what you hear.
Deployed, not assembledOne installer, one signed pack of modules, and every Pung you run at a glance on pung.live: its health, its modules, its updates.
Open and freeEvery module is built from its own source and keeps its licence. Pung itself is open source.
