For Ableton Push 3 Standalone

Music modules, at home on every device.

Pung adds 131 instruments, audio effects and MIDI generators to your Push 3. Each one is a device in Live, with a screen made for it.

Devices in LiveEvery module is in Push's browser, played with the pads, automated and saved with your Set. Nothing new to learn.
A screen made for itEnvelopes, filters and LFOs are drawn as the sound moves, and the classic synths get front panels of their own.
Open and freeEvery module is built from its own source and keeps its licence. Pung itself is open source.

Install

On your Push in a few minutes

The Pung Installer does everything from your computer: it finds your Push, installs what Pung needs and then Pung.

macOSApple silicon and IntelDownload WindowsWindows 10 and 11Download Linuxx86-64Download

  1. Open the installer and find your Push on your network.

  2. Let your computer in, once, with a key press on the Push.

  3. Install. Then add modules from Pung's page or from pung.live.

Every step, and what you need · Already on Push Hack? Install Pung from its catalog.

Pung installs through Push Hack, the open framework for community software on Push 3.

  1. Install Push Hack on your Push with the Push Hack Installer for macOS, Windows and Linux.

  2. Add Pung in Push Hack Catalog: open http://push.local:7702, find Pung and choose Install.

  3. Restart Live once on the Push. Then add modules from Pung's page or from pung.live.
