Takeover

Its surface on the Push is an app of its own: while the device is open, that app has the screen, the pads and the buttons. It needs that app installed; without it, Pung's own view opens.

On: a card over three columns shows the touched parameter. Off: the touched column shows its range, the neighbouring options and fine adjustment in place. The Push's columns are wide enough for off.

How it fits together

Pung devices are Live devices

A Pung device sits on a track like any instrument or effect. Live adds, removes, bypasses and saves it. This page and the Pung view on the Push edit the same device: a change here reaches Live, Push's own device view and automation.

On the Push: Shift + Help opens Home, then Pung.
