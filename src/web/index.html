Play a Schwung track from Live

  1. In Live's settings on the Push, MIDI: turn on Track (and Sync, for arpeggiators) for the output port Schwung.
  2. Turn on Link and Link Audio in Live.
  3. Make a MIDI track and send its MIDI to Schwung, on the channel of the Schwung track (channel 1 is track 1). Or press Make Live MIDI track above.
  4. Make an audio track with the input PushHack, channel Schwung N, Monitor In.

On the Push itself: Shift + Help opens Home, then Schwung.
